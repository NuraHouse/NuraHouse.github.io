Pause Together Privacy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Pause Together is created and published by Nura House. We care about privacy and aim to collect only the information necessary for the app and its supporting services to function.

Information you enter in Pause Together

Pause Together allows you to make selections about emotions, relationships, needs and communication.

These selections are designed to be processed on your device and are not sent to or stored on Nura House servers.

Pause Together does not require you to create a user account.

Advertising

Pause Together may display advertisements using Google AdMob, provided through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Google and its advertising services may automatically process certain device and usage information for purposes such as serving ads, measuring performance, preventing fraud and providing advertising-related analytics.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices and Google settings, this information may include device identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP address, interactions with ads or the app, and diagnostic information.

For more information about how Google handles information when its services are used in apps, please see Google Privacy Policy .

Consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, Pause Together may request your consent before serving certain types of advertising.

Advertising options may vary based on your region and the choices you make through Google's consent and privacy systems.

Information Nura House does not sell

Nura House does not sell the personal information you enter into Pause Together.

Data retention

Nura House does not maintain a server-side database containing your Pause Together emotion or relationship selections.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to those providers' own policies and retention practices.

Security

We aim to minimize the amount of information processed by the app and use established third-party services where necessary.

Third-party services

Pause Together may use:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising
  • Google User Messaging Platform where required for privacy consent

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pause Together changes, when we add or remove services, or when legal requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
