About us

Nura House is an independent studio founded by a couple who love turning everyday ideas into thoughtful digital experiences.

Most of our projects begin with a simple conversation about something that could make everyday life a little easier, calmer or more connected. We shape those ideas together and bring them to life with care.

Pause Together began with conversations between us about understanding each other a little better. Nudge grew from the simple idea that reminders should feel helpful, not overwhelming.

We create things inspired by everyday life, connection and the little details that can make a meaningful difference.
