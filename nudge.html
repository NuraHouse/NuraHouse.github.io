Nudge Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Nudge is created and published by Nura House. We respect your privacy and aim to process only the information needed to provide Nudge's reminder and sharing features.

Information you provide

Nudge may allow you to create reminders, notification preferences, schedules and other information you choose to enter into the app.

If you use account or family-sharing features, information necessary to operate those features may also be processed.

Reminders and notifications

Nudge uses notifications to deliver reminders you create. Notification delivery may depend on your device settings, operating system restrictions, battery settings and internet connection.

Family and shared reminders

If you choose to share reminders with another person, the selected reminder information may be made available to the recipient you choose.

You are responsible for the information you choose to share with other users through Nudge.

Data storage and synchronization

Some information may be stored locally on your device. Features such as account synchronization, family sharing or cross-device functionality may require information to be processed using cloud services.

This policy will be updated to identify the specific service providers used by Nudge before those services are made available in a public release.

Purchases

If Nudge offers paid features, purchases may be processed by the app store through which you obtained the application.

Nura House does not directly receive your full payment card details from the app store.

Health-related reminders

Nudge may allow you to create reminders related to medication, appointments or other health-related tasks.

Nudge is a general reminder and organization tool and is not a medical service.

You should not rely on Nudge as the sole method for remembering critical medical, emergency or safety-related tasks.

Information we do not sell

Nura House does not sell your personal information.

Children's privacy

Nudge is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children except where a feature is specifically designed and operated in accordance with applicable law.

Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Nudge.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Nudge develops, including when new synchronization, account, family, advertising or analytics services are introduced.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
