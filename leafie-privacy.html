Leafie Privacy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Leafie is created and published by Nura House. Leafie is designed around a simple idea: one real moment outside each day, captured through your camera and kept as part of your personal plant calendar.

Because photos can be personal, we believe it should be clear what Leafie accesses, what stays on your device, what may be processed by third-party services and what you choose to share.

This Privacy Policy explains how photos, optional captions, calendar information, notifications, advertising and other app features are handled.

Information you provide

Leafie may process information you choose to create within the app, including daily photos, optional photo captions, calendar entries, reminder preferences and app settings.

You control the content you choose to create and, where available, the content you choose to export or share.

Camera access

Leafie uses your device camera so you can take your daily plant or tree photo directly within the app.

The daily photo experience is designed around live camera capture. Gallery uploads are not used for confirming the daily Leafie photo.

Camera access is only used when required for features that need it, and access depends on the permissions you grant on your device.

Photos

Photos you take in Leafie are used to build your personal daily and monthly plant calendar.

In the current version of Leafie, your photos are designed to remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Nura House does not use your Leafie photos for advertising, profiling or sale.

Optional photo captions

After taking a photo, Leafie allows you to add an optional short caption or note before confirming the photo for that day.

These captions are treated as part of your personal Leafie content and remain associated with the relevant photo on your device.

Adding a caption is completely optional. Please avoid including sensitive personal information that you would not want stored together with your photo.

Daily photo and calendar data

Leafie records information needed to organize your personal calendar, such as the date associated with a confirmed daily photo and whether a day has been completed.

This information may also be used to provide features such as calendar history, discovery counts, streak information and monthly views.

Daily photo rule

Leafie is designed to allow one confirmed photo for each calendar day. Once you choose to add a photo to that day, it may be permanently locked within the app and cannot be replaced.

This restriction is part of Leafie's core daily experience and is intended to preserve an authentic record of each day.

Local storage

Leafie may store photos, captions, calendar information, app preferences and related data locally on your device.

If you remove Leafie, reset your device or lose local application data, locally stored content may also be lost unless you have separately exported or backed it up.

If cloud synchronization, accounts or online backup are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are made publicly available.

Notifications

Leafie may use notifications to remind you that your daily plant photo is waiting.

Notification delivery depends on your device settings, permissions and operating system behavior.

Leafie may stop or adjust daily reminder notifications after the day's photo has been completed.

Sharing and downloads

Leafie may allow you to download or share individual photos, calendar views, monthly collages or other content you create.

Sharing only happens when you choose to use a sharing, saving or export feature.

Once content is shared outside Leafie, it may be processed by the application, platform or service you choose to share it with.

Advertising

Leafie may display advertisements using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may automatically process certain device and usage information for purposes such as serving ads, measuring performance, preventing fraud and providing advertising-related analytics.

Depending on your device, region, privacy choices and advertising settings, this information may include device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please see Google Privacy Policy .

Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, Leafie may ask for your consent before certain types of advertising are served.

Advertising and privacy options may vary depending on your region, device and choices made through the advertising provider's consent and privacy systems.

Remove Ads purchases

Leafie may offer an optional purchase that allows you to remove advertisements from the app.

Purchases are processed by the app store through which you obtained Leafie, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Nura House does not directly receive your complete payment card information from the app marketplace.

Third-party services

Depending on the version and features available, Leafie may use third-party services for functions such as advertising, consent, purchases or app distribution.

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, if advertising is enabled
  • Google User Messaging Platform, where required for advertising consent
  • Google Play or Apple App Store services for purchases and app distribution

Information we do not sell

Nura House does not sell your Leafie photos, captions, calendar information or personal information.

Data retention

Locally stored Leafie content remains on your device according to the app's storage behavior and your device settings.

Content you export or share may remain outside Leafie according to the policies and storage behavior of the destination you selected.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to those providers' own retention practices and privacy policies.

Security

We aim to minimize the amount of information processed by Leafie and use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the app.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we design Leafie with data minimization and privacy in mind.

Children's privacy

Leafie is not specifically designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If Leafie's intended audience or features change in a way that requires additional protections for children, this policy and the app's privacy practices will be updated accordingly.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Leafie changes, when new features or third-party services are introduced, or when legal requirements change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
